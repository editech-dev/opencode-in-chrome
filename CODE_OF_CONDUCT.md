# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone contributing to opencode-in-chrome — issues, discussions, code, docs — must keep the
space welcoming and harassment-free.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; keep critique technical and actionable.
- No spam, trolling, personal attacks, or sharing others' private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hate speech, sexualized language, doxxing, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report violations to edisonisaza@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. Repeated or
severe violations result in a ban from the project's spaces.

This text follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant; it applies inside all project spaces
(repo, issues, discussions) and when representing the project publicly.
